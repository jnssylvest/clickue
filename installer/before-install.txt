Clickue - please read before installing
=======================================

Clickue is vibe-coded and was developed with substantial AI assistance. Caution was taken
during development, but AI-assisted development does not guarantee bug-free or secure
software. Review the source and use normal precautions. Test automations safely, protect
notification credentials, and avoid unattended use for consequential actions.

What Clickue does
- It clicks, types and presses keys in other programs on this computer, on a schedule or
  when something appears or disappears on screen. Test every job (Tests -> Dry run, Step
  through) before leaving it running.
- Everything stays on this computer. The only network use is the optional phone alerts
  (Discord / Telegram) that you set up yourself.
- Windows has one mouse and one keyboard: Clickue runs one job at a time and can't click
  while the screen is locked or a remote session is disconnected.
- Emergency stop: Ctrl+Alt+X (changeable) stops the running job, lets go of held keys and
  pauses everything.

Use it responsibly: follow your workplace's rules and the terms of the programs and sites
you automate. Clickue is not for games, scraping, or getting around CAPTCHAs or other
security checks.

Uninstalling: Settings -> Apps -> Clickue, or Clickue's own Menu -> Uninstall. You choose
whether your jobs and other data are kept or deleted.

Source code, guide and security notes: https://github.com/jnssylvest/clickue
